Trees can make a property cooler and more comfortable. Well-placed shade can reduce sun on roofs and walls, while healthy soil and vegetation help manage rainwater.

But a large tree close to the house can also bring risk. Falling limbs, roof contact, clogged drainage, and storm cleanup may outweigh some of the cooling benefit.

The best approach is not to remove every tree. It is to understand the species, condition, distance from the building, and the hazards common to the area.

Prune where needed, keep branches clear of rooflines, and have a qualified arborist assess concerning trees. Preserve useful shade when it can be maintained safely.

Keep an inventory, photos, and maintenance records. They show how the landscape supports comfort while managing storm and ownership exposure.
