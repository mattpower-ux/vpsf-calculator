During an outage, you may not need to power the entire house. Identify essential loads: refrigeration, communications, medical equipment, pumps, and perhaps heating or cooling.

A critical-load panel can separate those circuits so a battery or generator serves them efficiently. Solar panels may recharge a compatible battery, but solar alone does not necessarily provide power when the grid is down.

The choice depends on outage length, local hazards, fuel availability, and how much power each essential device uses. Safe transfer equipment and professional installation are crucial.

Reduce the load first, too. Insulation, shade, and efficient appliances help a limited backup supply last longer.

Document the protected circuits, battery capacity or generator rating, installation date, permits, and maintenance. That makes backup readiness a verifiable feature, not just a claim.
