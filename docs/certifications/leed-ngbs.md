# VPSF certification reference: LEED residential and NGBS Green

Date reviewed: **2026-11-15**. Scope: these two assigned program families only. Official program-owner/administrator sources only. Research reference; no product changes or deployment.

## Evidence rules

Certificates help discover missing physical features; they do **not** award blanket VPSF points. A level is not a package of identical equipment or credits.

- Record the exact program, edition, pathway, final award level, certificate/project ID, address and building/unit coverage, award date, and supporting report. Registration, preliminary ratings and precertification are not final awards.
- **Version unknown:** retain the certification claim and request the final report. Do not assign the newest edition from the award year or borrow requirements across editions. Preserve applicable addenda, substitutions, exceptions and alternative compliance paths.
- **Historical versus current:** certification documents establish evidence at verification, not continued presence or performance. Mark equipment as “documented at certification; current status unverified” until replacement history/current evidence confirms it. Never overwrite newer equipment data with an older report. Certification date is not roof installation date.
- **Report-only:** retain a precise fact with source, date and scope when VPSF has no exact field/value. Do not force broader requirements into narrower choices. Unknown/unearned credit is not evidence that a feature is absent. Avoid counting the same feature twice when multiple certificates verify it.

## LEED residential

### Versions, scope and levels

| Exact pathway | Scope and handling |
|---|---|
| LEED for Homes v2008; LEED for Homes Midrise Pilot v2010 | Legacy awards: retain their original system and scorecard. Base thresholds: Certified 45, Silver 60, Gold 75, Platinum 90; home-size adjustments can change thresholds. Do not apply v4 rules retrospectively. |
| LEED v4 BD+C: Homes and Multifamily Lowrise | Single-family attached/detached and multifamily 1–3 stories; certain 4–5-story projects qualify under stated exceptions. |
| LEED v4 BD+C: Multifamily Midrise | Multifamily 4+ stories. |
| LEED v4.1 Residential: Single Family | Attached/detached homes and multifamily up to four units, with individual unit registration/certification. |
| LEED v4.1 Residential: Multifamily / Multifamily Core and Shell | Multifamily 2+ units, any height, outside the US/Canada. Core and Shell excludes dwelling-unit space-conditioning systems and electrical appliances from the installation scope; it is not proof those systems exist. |

For v4/v4.1: **Certified 40–49; Silver 50–59; Gold 60–79; Platinum 80+**. See USGBC's [Residential certification guide](https://www.usgbc.org/tools/leed-certification/residential), “Register → Identify your rating system” and “Certify → Certification levels”; legacy adjustment details: [Homes rating system, April 2013 update](https://www.usgbc.org/sites/default/files/LEED%20for%20Homes%20Rating%20System_updated%20April%202013.pdf), “Home Size Adjuster.”

New construction and qualifying gut rehabilitation are distinct from partial remodeling: v4 eligibility describes exposure of wall/ceiling cavities for inspection. See [v4 Homes guide](https://www.usgbc.org/guide/homes), “LEED Work Plan,” steps 2–5. US/Canadian multifamily projects may use v4 with allowed v4.1 credit substitutions; record those substitutions individually. See [USGBC pathway clarification](https://usgbc.my.site.com/support/s/article/Choosing-between-LEED-for-Residential-and-LEED-for-Building-Design-and-Construction), “Can I use LEED v4.1 BD+C: NC…” (modified October 28, 2026).

**v5 boundary:** USGBC allows multifamily buildings with at least four above-grade floors to choose LEED v5 BD+C; buildings of three floors or fewer continue with applicable v4/v4.1 pathways. A residential-use building bearing v5 is not a v4.1 Residential certificate. Route its feature review to its actual BD+C report; v5 feature rules are not established by this reference. See [USGBC rating-system selection FAQ](https://usgbc.my.site.com/support/s/article/LEED-rating-system-selection), question on multifamily residential eligibility for v5.

### Mandatory requirements versus chosen credits

**v4:** applicable prerequisites must be met, plus category floors: 8 combined LT/EA points, 3 WE points and 3 EQ points. Consequently, “any credits totaling 40” is incomplete. Relevant prerequisites include minimum energy performance, ventilation, combustion venting, garage protection, radon-resistant construction and air filtering; applicability and exceptions matter. MERV 8 applies to recirculating systems, with separate nonducted/outdoor-air provisions—not every home has an identical filter. Heat Island Reduction, Enhanced Ventilation and Low-Emitting Products are credits, not universal features. [Official v4 rating system, July 25, 2019 compilation](https://www.usgbc.org/sites/default/files/LEED%20v4%20Homes_07.25.19_current.pdf), pp. 9, 26, 32, 76, 78–87, 100. Use the project's applicable addenda, not this compilation alone.

**v4.1 Single Family:** Water Use and Water Metering are prerequisites; enhanced ventilation and low-emitting products remain selectable credits. Do not carry v4 category floors or energy thresholds into v4.1 by assumption. [USGBC Single Family overview](https://www.usgbc.org/sites/default/files/2022-02/LEED%20v41%20Residential%20Single%20Family_Overview_SLIDES.pdf), slide 9, scorecard; [official Water Efficiency credit library](https://www.usgbc.org/credits?Category=%22Water+efficiency%22&Rating+System=New+Construction&Version=v4.1), filter to Residential Single Family. The ventilation prerequisite references ASHRAE 62.2-2016/local-equivalent requirements; this establishes no ERV requirement. [USGBC ventilation handout](https://www.usgbc.org/sites/default/files/2025-02/HANDOUT%20Redcalc%20ASHRAE%2062_2%20Vent%20Calcs.pdf), opening “Ventilation—Required / Requirements.”

**Report request:** final awarded scorecard, rating-system version/addenda and substituted credit versions, Green Rater verification, energy model/rating, installed equipment schedules, water calculator/fixture inventory, landscape plan and product documentation. A selected credit may contain alternative options: obtain the **awarded option**, not merely its title. For example, enhanced whole-house ventilation can document balanced ventilation, while another ventilation option concerns bathroom exhaust controls. Water testing documents leaks at testing, not an automatic shutoff. [USGBC Verification and Submittals, August 2025](https://www.usgbc.org/sites/default/files/2025-10/LEED%20v4%20Homes%20and%20Multifamily%20Midrise%20and%20LEED%20v4.1%20Residential%20Single%20Family%20Verification%20and%20Submittals_8-12-25.pdf), pp. 7–8, 17, 20–24 (printed page labels).

## NGBS Green

### Editions, scope and levels

ICC 700 editions include **2008, 2012, 2015, 2020 and 2025**. Treat the first three as historical rule sets; current program material identifies certification under **2020 and 2025**. Do not substitute the edition's publication year for a property's certification date. Sources: [Home Innovation edition history](https://www.ngbs.com/documents/NGBS-GCP-Overview-and-Application-Resources.pdf), “Certification Fundamentals,” p. 7; [current program edition selector](https://www.ngbs.com/green-certified-products/search-green-products), introductory scope statement. Older pages still mentioning only 2015/2020 are insufficient evidence of current availability.

| Pathway | Award and boundary |
|---|---|
| New single-family/townhouse/duplex; multifamily and residential mixed-use | Bronze, Silver, Gold, Emerald under the applicable edition and category requirements. |
| Chapter 12 streamlined single-family path (2020; retained in 2025) | **Certified**: all applicable path practices, rather than a Bronze-to-Emerald points selection. Includes townhouses/duplexes. Do not interpret “Certified” as another name for Bronze. |
| Existing buildings, Chapter 11 | Bronze, Silver, Gold, Emerald; renovation-specific requirements. New-construction equipment rules cannot be universally applied to retained equipment. |
| Land development | One–Four Stars; certification is independent of the buildings on the land. |
| 2025 property certification | Bronze–Emerald; multi-building scope, with category/path-dependent aggregation. Establish the subject building's inclusion and its own feature evidence. |

Official scope sections: [Single-Family Homes](https://www.ngbs.com/get-certified/certification-types-levels/single-family-certification), “Single-Family NGBS Green Certification”; [2025 Chapter 12 interpretation](https://www.ngbs.com/resources/ngbs-interpretations?filter=chapter-12), “2025 NGBS…Chapter 12…1203 ENERGY EFFICIENCY”; [Existing Building Certification](https://www.ngbs.com/get-certified/certification-types-levels/existing-building-certification), body text; [Land Development](https://www.ngbs.com/get-certified/certification-types-levels/land-development), “Four levels”; [Property](https://www.ngbs.com/get-certified/certification-types-levels/property), “One Property, Multiple Buildings…”. Commercial Core and Shell/Full Mixed-Use “Certified” and product recognition are separate scopes, not a home award: [Certification Types & Levels](https://www.ngbs.com/get-certified/certification-types-levels), “NGBS Green Certification” and “Giving Products Our Green Approval.”

### Credit choice and mandatory requirements

The standard points pathway requires **all applicable mandatory provisions, each category minimum, and additional points**. For 2020 new construction:

| Category | Bronze | Silver | Gold | Emerald |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Lot design | 50 | 64 | 93 | 121 |
| Resource efficiency | 43 | 59 | 89 | 119 |
| Energy | 30 | 45 | 60 | 70 |
| Water | 25 | 39 | 67 | 92 |
| Indoor environmental quality | 25 | 42 | 69 | 97 |
| Operations/education | 8 | 10 | 11 | 12 |
| Additional, any category | 50 | 75 | 100 | 100 |
| Total | 231 | 334 | 489 | 611 |

Homes over 4,000 sq. ft. require additional points under §601.1. This is not a Chapter 11/12 or land-development threshold table. Source: [Home Innovation 2020 comparison](https://www.ngbs.com/documents/08-ngbs-leedmultifamily-41comparison2020.pdf), printed p. 5, “Threshold Point Ratings.” Use this source for its own NGBS table, not its broad claims of equivalence with LEED. The administrator reports unchanged Bronze–Emerald point thresholds in 2025, but requirements changed: [2025 changes](https://www.ngbs.com/resources/articles/summary-key-changes-in-the-2025-ngbs/46140), “Compliance Methods.”

**2020 new construction:** mandatory practices cover moisture detailing, energy compliance, pollutant control and owner information. Whole-building ventilation under §902.2.1 is mandatory below 5 ACH50; alternatives include exhaust/supply, balanced, HRV and ERV. Bathroom exhaust is required; dryer venting has a condensing-dryer exception. These are not blanket ERV or all-electric rules. See [Builder's Resource Guide, October 2026](https://www.ngbs.com/documents/ngbs-builders-resource-guide.pdf), Appendix B, PDF pp. 39–49, especially p. 46 (§§902.1.1–902.2.1); Appendix C separates 2025 requirements. PDF page numbers here are one-based physical pages, not appendix labels.

**2025 new construction:** changes include whole-building ventilation regardless of leakage, outdoor kitchen exhaust, ventilation airflow testing and mandatory MERV 8+ filters. Resilience practices and points for all-electric buildings do not make those optional strategies universal. [2025 changes](https://www.ngbs.com/resources/articles/summary-key-changes-in-the-2025-ngbs/46140), Chapters 6, 7 and 9. Resolve exact exceptions/path applicability in the final report before assigning a feature.

**Report request:** final verifier scoring report with edition, chapter/path and individual practice/subpractice selections; energy compliance calculation, equipment inventory, water/landscape schedules and owner manual. Energy has performance, prescriptive, ERI-target and alternative routes; level alone supplies neither HVAC type nor a numeric HERS score. [Home Innovation 2020 energy requirements](https://www.ngbs.com/documents/Comparison-NGBS-vs-Earth-Advantage.pdf), “Energy Efficiency Requirements,” §§701.1.4, 702–704. Obtain separate evidence for WaterSense or NGBS Green+ achievements; ordinary NGBS certification is not proof of either. [Water Efficiency Certifications](https://www.ngbs.com/get-certified/water-efficiency-certifications), WaterSense/WRI sections.

## VPSF field decisions — both families

These are conservative mapping rules, not additional certification requirements. **Neither family name nor award level alone populates any of the physical fields below.** Verified, applicable prerequisites may be described narrowly in the reference report; selected credits require project evidence.

| VPSF field(s) | Evidence that can populate a matching value; otherwise report-only |
|---|---|
| `hvac`, `hers` | Installed system/fuel/model and actual dated HERS result for this home. Energy savings, an ERI alternative or a modeled building average is not a numeric home HERS score or proof of a heat pump. |
| `ventilation` (Balanced/ERV) | Explicit installed balanced-system evidence supports Balanced; explicit ERV evidence supports ERV. Required ventilation, exhaust fans and HRVs do not establish ERV. Preserve other configurations as report-only if unrepresented. |
| `materials` (Low-VOC), `iaq` | Actual awarded product category, applicable emissions/VOC criterion and installed coverage; IAQ equipment/test results with scope. A paint credit is not all materials; filtration, radon detailing or a past test is not a generic whole-home IAQ guarantee. |
| `waterStandard`, `leak` (Auto) | Exact named certification or documented fixture standard with matching field meaning. Populate Auto only with evidence of an installed automatic shutoff covering the relevant supply. Metering, leak-free pressure tests, leak alerts or irrigation master valves alone are insufficient. |
| `reuse`, `landscape` | Installed rain/graywater reuse system and its use, or documented plant/irrigation strategy. Water reductions, a water-budget calculation or no-invasive-plants requirement does not uniquely identify these features. |
| `roof`, `roofAge`, `siding` | Actual material and installation/replacement records. Heat-island performance, durability, recycled content and Class A fire ratings do not establish metal roofing or a siding type. |
| `fortified`, `wildfireMitigation`, `windProtection`, `temperatureProtection` | Exact separate designation or installed protective measure, location and applicable hazard. A resilience credit, general durability or energy efficiency is insufficient. Unrepresented measures remain report-only. |
| `daylighting`, `carbonStrategy` | Specific installed daylighting design or documented strategy matching the field. Windows, energy points, high award level or reduced modeled emissions do not establish daylighting, onsite solar, electrification or net-zero status. |

For an older certificate with no current inspection, keep discovered features as historical candidates for confirmation. For an unidentified edition/path, stop at certification metadata and a report request. No inferred “missing feature” becomes an installed feature merely because it is common in certified projects.
