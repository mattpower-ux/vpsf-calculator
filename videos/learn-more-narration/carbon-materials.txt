A home's climate impact does not begin when the lights turn on. Building products carry emissions from extraction, manufacturing, transport, installation, and eventual replacement.

Judge materials over their useful life, not by one label. A durable, repairable product may save materials and emissions over time.

Environmental Product Declarations, often called E P Ds, provide standardized information for comparing the impacts of similar products. They are evidence, not a shortcut around good design.

Choose materials suited to local moisture, fire, and weather conditions. Consider lower-carbon concrete, responsibly sourced wood, recycled content, and verified insulation when they fit the job.

Keep product declarations, specifications, warranties, and replacement records. Documentation makes the home's carbon story more credible and useful to future buyers.
