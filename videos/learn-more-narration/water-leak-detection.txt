A hidden plumbing leak can damage walls, floors, and finishes. Preventing damage matters as much as saving water.

Whole-home leak detection watches for unusual flow or moisture. Some systems alert the owner; others can close an automatic shutoff valve before a problem spreads.

Place sensors where leaks are most likely to go unnoticed, such as near water heaters, washing machines, sinks, and mechanical equipment. The main valve should be accessible and the system should be tested after installation.

No sensor replaces good plumbing or regular inspection. But early detection can limit wasted water, reduce repair work, and protect materials already in the home.

Keep the model, installation date, shutoff details, and test records. That evidence helps buyers understand what protection is actually in place, and may be useful in insurance conversations.
