The purchase price is only one part of what a home will cost. Roofs, heating systems, water heaters, and other major components eventually need service or replacement.

When their age and condition are unknown, buyers face surprise expenses. A simple inventory of equipment, installation dates, and maintenance history makes those future costs easier to plan.

Insurance exposure also depends on local hazards and the home's condition. A well-documented roof, leak protection, or storm upgrade may help explain risk, though discounts vary by insurer and location.

Prioritize work that prevents damage or keeps essential systems reliable before spending on optional cosmetic changes. Timely maintenance can extend service life and avoid unnecessary waste.

Save permits, invoices, warranties, inspections, and a realistic replacement timeline. Those records turn ownership risk into a clearer, more useful value story.
