Water heating runs all year, so an efficient upgrade matters even when heating and cooling are idle.

A heat pump water heater moves heat from surrounding air into the tank. That usually takes less electricity than a standard resistance element. Hybrid models can use backup heat during high demand.

Installation matters too. The unit needs enough air, service space, a safe condensate drain, and a tank sized for the household. Its cooling and sound may affect nearby rooms.

Smart scheduling can shift heating toward lower-cost hours or times when rooftop solar is producing. A drain pan, leak sensor, and shutoff add protection against water damage.

Keep the model number, efficiency rating, installation date, and permits. Those details turn a promised upgrade into something a future buyer can verify.
