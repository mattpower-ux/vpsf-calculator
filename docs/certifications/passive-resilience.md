# Passive-building and resilience certification reference

Reviewed: **2026-11-15**. Scope: **Phius and Passive House Institute (PHI), separately; FORTIFIED Home; Wildfire Prepared Home**. Primary sources only. Research reference, not implementation or a scoring rule.

## Evidence rules

- Use certification to discover missing physical features, **never blanket certification points**. A program name alone records recognition; the rules below require a matching final certificate, address, scope, edition/path/level, and evidence date. Design certification, registration, a retrofit plan, or a certified product is not completed-home certification.
- **Version unknown:** keep recognition, request the certificate and project report, and leave physical fields unresolved. Do not apply today's requirements retroactively. Historical editions are a deliberate coverage gap in this reference; their requirements must be checked when an actual certificate is supplied.
- **Old versus current equipment:** certification documents establish the certified configuration. Replacement HVAC, ventilators, roof coverings, windows, additions, or altered landscaping need current evidence. Certificate date is neither equipment installation date nor roof age. Preserve historical facts separately; do not overwrite newer evidence.
- **Report-only** means retain the precise finding in supporting documentation because the existing field has no exact representation. Do not round it into a stronger option. Missing evidence means unknown, not absent.

## 1. Phius — separate from PHI

**Edition reviewed:** *Phius 2024 Passive Building Standards, Certification Guidebook v26.1.2*, October 2026. “2024” is the standard family; “26.1.2” is the guidebook revision. Sources: [current guidebook landing page](https://www.phius.org/phius-certification-guidebook) and [official v26.1.2 guidebook](https://www.phius.org/sites/default/files/2026-10/Phius%20Certification%20Guidebook%20v26.1.2%20%281%29.pdf), sections cited below.

| Path / scope | Mandatory versus optional; interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| **CORE 2024 Performance** | Residential, mixed-use and commercial; new construction and retrofit subject to scope rules. Requires project-specific heating/cooling demand and load limits, source-energy limits, airtightness, moisture/comfort provisions and mechanical ventilation. It is a requirements-based program, not a credit tally. Renewable generation is not universally required. §§0.6.1.1, 1.3.1–1.3.5. |
| **CORE 2024 Prescriptive** | New single-family, side-by-side duplexes and townhouses in the US/Canada; not stacked units. Climate/building-specific envelope and mechanical requirements with defined tradeoffs; renewable generation receives no credit on this path. Fossil-fuel equipment/appliances prohibited; qualifying biomass appliances permitted. §§2.3.1–2.3.6, 2.4.3. |
| **ZERO 2024 Performance** | CORE conservation requirements plus modeled annual net-zero source energy. On-site and off-site renewables may be combined. Fossil-fuel equipment/appliances/fireplaces prohibited, with specified exceptions for emergency electricity generation and exterior cooking. Neither rooftop PV nor a particular heat pump follows from the label. §§0.6.1.2, 1.3.5.2, 1.3.6. |
| **Retrofit / REVIVE** | Current guidebook §3.0 says **REVIVE 2024 Pilot is not accepting submissions**; existing pilot teams use Appendix R. New retrofits follow CORE/ZERO performance rules (§3.3), including additions of any size and approved existing-condition allowances (§3.4). Pilot CORE/ZERO REVIVE has its own scope, including additions ≤20%, geographically conditional hazard requirements and optional electives (§§R-3.1–R-3.6). Do not transfer pilot resilience requirements to every Phius home. |

**Useful requirements and mapping limits:**

- Airtightness is area-based, not a universal PHI-style ACH50 threshold. Performance limits are 0.06 CFM50/ft² enclosure for most buildings, 0.08 for qualifying ≥5-story noncombustible buildings; prescriptive limit is 0.04. Preserve test pressure, denominator, result and boundary in the report. These limits are not a `hers` score. §§1.3.3.2, 2.3.3.1.
- **Do not automatically set `ventilation=Balanced` or `ERV`.** §1.3.4.2 permits supply-only/exhaust-only configurations with dedicated makeup openings; compliance may use measured supply/exhaust within 10% or net pressure imbalance ≤5 Pa. Prescriptive §[C]7 refers back to these rules, with climate-specific recovery requirements; §[C]7.2.2 expressly says ERV is not required in all climates. Use as-built system identity and commissioning to select Balanced or ERV/HRV accurately.
- Residential co-requisites in Table 1.5.3.0 include ENERGY STAR SFNH v3.2 / MFNC v1.2, Indoor AirPlus v1 rev.4, and Efficient New Homes single-family v2 rev.1 / multifamily v2. Footnotes and location/scope exceptions matter: multifamily ENH intent is required without universally requiring its certificate; retrofit checklists are completed “as if” certified (§3.5.3). Do not manufacture separate certifications or generalize Indoor AirPlus to all existing finishes.
- `temperatureProtection`: verified completed airtight enclosure supports an **Air Sealing** finding; use installed assembly documentation before selecting **Air Sealing + Insulation**, especially for allowed retrofit conditions. No outage-survival duration follows from ordinary CORE/ZERO compliance.
- `hvac`, numeric `hers`, `materials=Low-VOC`, `iaq` monitoring and particular resilience measures require their own evidence. ZERO's energy balance is **report-only** for `carbonStrategy`; it does not establish “Zero Carbon Certified,” material EPDs or whole-life carbon neutrality.

**Request:** final certification (not design approval), applicable guidebook/path, as-built model or prescriptive checklist, installed equipment schedules, ventilation commissioning, blower-door report, co-requisite checklists and accepted exceptions. For REVIVE, obtain the actual completed phase/hazard-mitigation reports; planned or elective measures are not installed features. See §§1.1.5–1.1.7, 1.4.2, 1.5.1–1.5.3, R-5–R-6.

**Version gap:** older PHIUS+/Phius 2015/2018/2021 and earlier 2024 guidebook revisions are not mapped here. Do not infer their co-requisites or equipment rules from v26.1.2.

## 2. Passive House Institute (PHI)

**Edition reviewed:** *Criteria for Buildings: Passive House – EnerPHit – PHI Low Energy Building*, **v10c, revised 2023-01-20, valid with PHPP 10**, still linked by PHI's [building certification page](https://passivehouse.com/en/home/building-certification/). [Official criteria PDF](https://univention.passiv.de/nextcloud/s/version10c-2023/download). Page references below use the compact version's printed pages; the same section numbers recur in the extended version with additional binding provisions.

| Standard / scope | Mandatory versus optional |
| --- | --- |
| **Passive House: Classic / Plus / Premium** | Same core efficiency and airtightness requirements across classes: heating demand ≤15 kWh/(m²·year) **or** heating load ≤10 W/m²; n50 ≤0.6 h⁻¹; climate-adjusted cooling limits. Baseline PER limits 60/45/30 kWh/(m²·year); renewable generation minima none/60/120, using **projected footprint**, not treated floor area. Table footnotes allow specified alternatives/tradeoffs; do not treat these headline numbers as each project's measured results. §2.1, Table 1, p.10. |
| **EnerPHit: Classic / Plus / Premium** | Existing-building retrofit, via either component criteria or energy-demand criteria; climate-dependent limits and permitted exceptions. General airtightness limit n50 ≤1.0 h⁻¹. Do not substitute Passive House's 0.6 limit or demand limits. Entirely new buildings cannot receive EnerPHit. §§2.2–2.2.4, pp.11–14. |
| **PHI Low Energy Building** | Distinct, less stringent standard, not Passive House Classic: heating demand ≤30 kWh/(m²·year), n50 ≤1.0 h⁻¹ and baseline PER ≤75, with stated alternatives. §2.3, Table 5, p.15. |

All are requirements-based rather than elective point schemes. Plus/Premium renewable requirements do **not** prove rooftop solar, battery storage or carbon-neutral materials: qualifying off-site generation ownership is permitted (§3.2.9, p.33). Classic does not prove that renewables are absent.

**Field use:** completed envelope evidence can support `temperatureProtection=Air Sealing`; insulation assemblies need the project booklet before selecting the combined option. Ventilation compliance alone does not identify an ERV, moisture recovery, or the exact VPSF system category. Populate `ventilation` and `hvac` only from equipment and commissioning records (§§2.4.3, 3.2.6–3.2.7, pp.16–17, 31–32). Airtightness, PHPP energy demand, thermal comfort and acoustic criteria are **report-only** where no exact field exists; none is a HERS result, IAQ sensor, daylighting result, or power-outage guarantee. `carbonStrategy` is report-only for PER/renewable balance.

**Request:** certificate plus accompanying building booklet, PHPP verification and scope, drawings/assembly schedules, ventilation adjustment report, equipment data, airtightness results and documented exemptions (§3.2, pp.28–36). EnerPHit staged-retrofit **pre-certification is not final completed retrofit certification** (§3.3, pp.37–38). The current PHI page also describes pilot EnerPHit Unit certification: a unit can have a hypothetical future central heat-pump supply for PER assessment; never convert that into installed `hvac` for the unit or whole building.

**Version/current-condition gap:** §3.1.3 (p.26) ties criteria to the start of energy planning, not award year alone. The official page archives v10b, v10a and v9 editions; these are not mapped here. Under §3.1.2 (p.26), certificate validity is tied to the documented construction/use and can be invalidated by relevant conversions, changed use or shading. Do not presume old equipment remains installed.

## 3. FORTIFIED Home — peril and level must both be known

**Edition reviewed:** [2025 FORTIFIED Home Standard](https://fortifiedhome.org/wp-content/uploads/2025-FORTIFIED-Home-Standard.pdf). The [official technical-document page](https://fortifiedhome.org/2025-technical-documents/) requires it for permits from **2025-11-01**, or roof installation from that date where no roof permit is required. Scope is eligible single-/two-family residential dwelling types under §2.2; Commercial and Multifamily have separate standards. **Hurricane** and **High Wind** are distinct designations; **Hail** is an optional supplement. Roof/Silver/Gold are cumulative requirement levels, not point totals (§§1.1–1.3, pp.5–9).

| Level | Mandatory features / peril distinction | Safe VPSF treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FORTIFIED Roof**, New Roof or Existing Roof | Applicable roof-deck attachment/strengthening and sealing, covering and vent requirements; paths differ (§§3–4). Does not establish whole-wall strengthening or continuous load path. | Precise assembly measures are **report-only**. `roof` material needs the evaluator's record. Wind-resistant roof ≠ hail-impact-rated roof. |
| **Silver — both perils** | Roof requirements plus applicable pressure-rated garage doors, gable-end sheathing/bracing, chimney and attached-structure provisions (§5). Gable-end protection is not all exterior walls. | Do not infer Gold's whole-building load path, siding material or all-window protection. |
| **Silver — Hurricane** | Adds impact protection for glazed openings and entry doors (§§5.2–5.3, pp.59–60); can use rated assemblies **or** qualifying protection systems. Stated exceptions apply. High Wind does not inherit these Hurricane-only rules. | Can support `windProtection=Rated Opening Protection` when certificate scope and evaluator evidence establish the applicable protection. Does not identify impact glass versus shutters. |
| **Gold — both perils** | Roof + Silver; exterior wall system with at least the pressure/debris resistance of 7/16-inch wood structural panels, plus roof-to-wall-to-foundation continuous load path (§§6.1, 6.3–6.5, pp.70 onward). | Wall resistance is **report-only**; it is not siding composition or fire resistance. For `Engineered Continuous Load Path`, obtain engineering/compliance evidence identifying the chosen new/retrofit path. |
| **Gold — Hurricane** | Adds pressure-rated openings under §6.2, with site-specific requirements and exceptions (p.70). High Wind Gold does not imply Hurricane-only window/entry-door ratings. | Record exact opening ratings in report; avoid “hurricane-proof” or safe-room claims. |
| **Hail supplement** | Optional to select, mandatory §7 requirements once designated: qualifying roof covering and, where present, skylights/PV. Asphalt shingles require IBHS Good/Excellent rating at installation; other materials have different test criteria (§§7.1–7.5). | Supports an impact-performance finding for the documented covering; select `Impact-Rated Roof` only where that option exists. Material and ratings remain separate. Never infer Hail from Roof/Silver/Gold alone. |

**Roof versus wall:** Gold's wall sheathing/performance requirement does not require metal, masonry or fiber-cement siding. Silver's gable-wall measures do not establish Gold's exterior-wall requirement. Roof alone establishes neither. FORTIFIED is about wind/wind-driven rain and optional hail, not wildfire, flood, indoor temperature or FEMA/ICC safe rooms (§1.3).

**Age and evidence:** designations last five years (§1.2, p.8). “Existing Roof” requires condition/remaining useful life; it does not say when installed. “New Roof” is a designation category with eligibility/time limits, not an exact installation date (§1.1, p.6). Request certificate with peril/level/Hail/roof category, evaluator report/photos, roof installation record, product ratings and Silver/Gold compliance forms. Populate `roofAge` only from dated installation evidence, not certificate issuance. `fortified` may retain exact recognition, without points or forced translation to a legacy option.

**Version gap:** 2020 and older standards, legacy Bronze labels, and project-specific technical bulletins are not fully mapped here. Preserve the original label/edition; do not silently relabel Bronze as 2025 Roof. Unknown peril blocks Hurricane-specific deductions; unknown level blocks Silver/Gold deductions.

## 4. IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home

**Edition reviewed:** [December 2025 Technical Standard](https://wildfireprepared.org/wp-content/uploads/WFPH-Technical-Standard-12-25.pdf), **effective 2026-06-01** per the [official standard page](https://wildfireprepared.org/home-technical-standard/), plus the 2026 bulletins below. Levels: **Base (Essential)** and **Plus (Enhanced)**. Each requires all applicable measures, not optional credit accumulation. Home and Multifamily are separate programs; confirm dwelling eligibility and whole-building/parcel scope (§1.1.3, p.4).

| Level | Exact roof / siding distinction |
| --- | --- |
| **Base / Essential** | Class A roof requirement (§2.1.1, p.8). Every exterior wall needs at least **six vertical inches of noncombustible material at its base**, measured from grade and the nearest horizontal surface, including decks/patios (§2.1.4, p.9). **This does not require all siding to be noncombustible.** |
| **Plus / Enhanced** | Base plus **noncombustible exterior wall coverings**, including the standard's column/beam/decorative-feature provisions (§3.1.4, p.14), subject to the narrowly defined bulletin allowances below. Vinyl and wood siding—including ignition-resistant treated/coated wood—do not satisfy that covering requirement. |

Both levels also address vents and defensible space (§§2.1.3, 2.2); Plus adds further envelope/attachment measures (§3). The designation term is three years, with annual reviews in the first two years (§1.1.4, pp.5–6).

**2026 allowances matter:**

- [WP 2026-01, Decorative Gable and Eave Framing Members](https://wildfireprepared.org/wp-content/uploads/TechnicalBulletin-DecorativeWoodElements.pdf), released **2026-08-29**, pp.1–3: permits specified decorative solid wood/mass-timber members at least **2 inches actual thickness**, attached to noncombustible cladding, with noncombustible protection over ember-collecting top surfaces, no ember-trapping concealed cavities, and compliant roof/eaves/soffits. Excludes the bulletin's listed hollow/foam/composite members and attached occupancy/vegetation-support structures. Obtain plans or field verification. This is not permission for wood siding.
- [WP 2026-02, Stucco-Coated Foam Shutters and Architectural Features](https://wildfireprepared.org/wp-content/uploads/WP-TB-Stucco-Coated-Foam-Shutters-and-Architectural-Features.pdf), released **2026-08-26**, pp.1–2: an alternative for isolated, fully coated features, ≥6 inches above relevant horizontal surfaces, separated from eaves/soffits/overhangs by compliant cladding, with ASTM E2707 or SFM 12-7A-1 evaluation. ASTM E84 alone is insufficient. Continuous foam wall coverings/EIFS, bands/cornices and exposed foam are excluded. Request product/coating/test evidence.

**Field use:** a matching, active, documented designation can support `wildfireMitigation=Ember Hardening + Defensible Space`; retain the actual vent method, clearances and inspection date. Class A fire performance and the six-inch strip are **report-only**, not `roof=Metal` or a whole-wall `siding` material. Plus establishes the covering performance category, not whether the home uses brick, stucco, metal or fiber cement; actual `siding` needs photos/specifications. Mixed materials and bulletin-compliant decorative elements must not be flattened into one invented material. Defensible space does not establish `landscape=Drought-Tolerant`.

**Request:** certificate with level/edition and current annual-review status, inspection/photos, roof assembly evidence, siding identity, and any applicable bulletin documentation. Do not infer current maintenance from an expired designation, roof installation age, a fireproof structure, indoor air filtration/monitoring, or wind/hail resistance.

**Version gap:** pre-June-2026 designations and earlier guides are not mapped here. Preserve their actual governing version and confirm requirements before using either today's siding or decorative-feature rules.

## Shared exclusions and next-document prompts

| Existing field(s) | Rule across this reference |
| --- | --- |
| `hvac`, `ventilation`, `hers` | Ask for current equipment/commissioning and an actual HERS report. Energy performance or required ventilation does not identify equipment, ERV moisture recovery or a HERS band. |
| `roof`, `roofAge`, `siding` | Ask for material/assembly and installation records. Fire rating, wind resistance, impact rating and wall sheathing are separate facts. |
| `materials`, `iaq`, `daylighting` | Ask for product emissions/finish scope, installed monitoring and daylighting evidence. Ventilation/comfort compliance does not establish these narrower fields. |
| `carbonStrategy` | Operational/source-energy compliance is not embodied-carbon documentation or a separate zero-carbon certification. Keep unmatched findings report-only. |
| `waterStandard`, `leak`, `reuse`, `landscape` | None of these program names alone proves WaterSense/HERS H2O/WERS, automatic shutoff, water reuse or drought-tolerant planting. Even a separately documented leak-free test would not establish automatic shutoff. |
| `fortified`, `wildfireMitigation`, `temperatureProtection`, `windProtection` | Preserve recognition without points. Populate only the supported physical feature; keep measures without an exact option report-only. Never infer a FEMA/ICC safe room from these credentials. |

These are evidence-discovery rules. No product code, scoring, deployment, or automatic import behavior is changed by this document.
