As homes get tighter, fresh air should not depend on leaks. Intentional ventilation controls how outdoor air enters.

A balanced system supplies outdoor air while removing stale indoor air. An H R V transfers some heat between those air streams. An E R V can also transfer some moisture, which may help in certain climates.

The design depends on the home and local humidity. Measure airflow, maintain filters, and keep outdoor intakes away from pollutants.

Ventilation works best alongside kitchen and bath exhaust, moisture control, and low-emitting materials. It cannot solve a damp wall or a combustion problem by itself.

Record the system type, airflow settings, service dates, and filter details. Those facts make a healthy-home claim more than a slogan.
