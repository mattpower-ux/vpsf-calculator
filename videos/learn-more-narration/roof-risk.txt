A roof is more than the visible shingles. It is a system of covering, underlayment, flashing, drainage, and attachment that keeps weather out of the house.

Age matters, but local wind, hail, heat, trees, installation quality, and maintenance also affect remaining life and risk.

When replacement is due, choose materials and attachment details suited to the actual hazards. Impact resistance may matter in hail country; secure edges and deck connections matter where strong winds are common.

Good flashing and water management protect insulation and finishes below. A durable roof also avoids repeated replacement waste and may support an insurance discussion, depending on the policy and market.

Save permits, invoices, product ratings, photos, and the installation date. They help the next owner understand what was installed and why.
